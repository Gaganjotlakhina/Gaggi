Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Assi20Tuc20 ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights under Canada's privacy laws (PIPEDA).

1. What we collect

  • Account info: username, password (stored as a one-way hash — we can't read it), and optional gender/country you provide.
  • Google sign-in: if you use it, we receive your verified email address and a Google account identifier.
  • Coins & transactions: your balance and a ledger of coin movements (purchases, gifts, bonuses, promo redemptions).
  • Reports: when you report someone (or are reported), we store the report to enforce bans.
  • Technical: basic server logs (IP address, timestamps) for security and abuse prevention.

What we don't collect: we do not record your video, audio, or chat messages. Calls are peer-to-peer; text chat passes through our server only to deliver it and isn't stored.

2. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see, store, or touch your card number — it goes directly to Stripe under their privacy policy.

3. Why we collect it

  • To run the Service: accounts, matching, gifts, purchases.
  • To keep the community safe: reports, bans, fraud prevention.
  • To comply with the law.

4. Sharing

We don't sell your data. We share limited data only with:

  • Stripe — to process payments.
  • Google — if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Law enforcement — where required by law, particularly for the safety of minors.

5. Storage & retention

Data is stored on secure servers in Canada/US. We keep account data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time (see below); some records (e.g. payment ledgers, abuse reports) may be kept as required by law.

6. Your rights

Under PIPEDA you can request access to your personal information, ask us to correct it, or withdraw consent / delete your account. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies & local storage

We store a login session token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. No advertising trackers.

8. Children

Assi20Tuc20 is 18+ only. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; accounts found to belong to minors are deleted.

9. Changes

We'll post policy updates here with a new "last updated" date.
